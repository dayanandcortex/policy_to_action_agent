Care Classic Health Insurance Policy

This policy covers hospitalization expenses incurred due to illness or accident, subject to the terms and conditions below.

1. Initial Waiting Period
Any hospitalization within 30 days from the policy start date shall not be covered, except for accidental injuries.

2. Pre-existing Diseases
Expenses related to pre-existing diseases are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.

3. Day Care Procedures
Day care treatments are covered if they do not require 24-hour hospitalization and are listed in the approved procedure annexure.

4. Room Rent
Room rent is covered up to 1% of the sum insured per day.

5. Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery, fertility treatment, and non-medically necessary procedures are excluded from coverage unless specifically endorsed.

6. Claim Review
Claims with incomplete documentation or unclear medical necessity may be sent for manual review.
